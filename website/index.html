Questions, answered

Is Tempa a free planner app?

Yes — Tempa is free to download and use for core planning. Tempa Plus unlocks unlimited AI planning, focus sessions, and more — with a free trial before anything is charged.

Is Tempa a good planner for ADHD?

Yes. Tempa is designed ADHD-first — one thing at a time, voice capture, gentle nudges. But it works for anyone whose day never fits a rigid planner: restless minds, busy parents, creatives, students.

Does Tempa work as a Pomodoro / focus timer?

Yes. Focus mode has a built-in timer: set the dial, start a session, and Tempa keeps just one task in front of you until the timer ends.

What happens to my voice recordings?

Voice input is transcribed to create your tasks and is not stored longer than needed for that. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Which devices are supported?

Tempa is available for iPhone (iOS 17 and later). iPad and Android are on the roadmap.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the App Store. Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, choose Tempa, and tap Cancel. You keep access until the end of the paid period.
