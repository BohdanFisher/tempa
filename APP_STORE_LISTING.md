## Description

Tempa is a voice-first day planner for ADHD adults — and for anyone whose to-do
list has quietly turned into a wall of guilt. Say your day out loud, and Tempa's
AI turns it into a real schedule: times, durations, and an order that fits how
your energy actually moves.

Then it does the part that matters most. It hides the wall and shows you one
thing — the one you're doing right now.

No streaks to lose. No red overdue badges. No screen telling you how far behind
you are. When a day falls apart, Tempa reshapes it and moves on with you.

Planning in Tempa is free. Tempa Pro is an auto-renewable subscription that
unlocks the AI features, and new subscribers get 3 days free on the yearly plan.

Key Features:

Speak Your Day (Pro): Talk the way you think — "gym at eight, deep work until
lunch, call Mum in the evening." Tempa writes the schedule; you tap once to keep
it.

One Thing at a Time: The Right Now block shows the single task you're on, with
everything else deliberately out of sight until it's their turn.

Break It Down (Pro): Stuck on something big? Tempa splits it into steps small
enough to actually start, so beginning costs almost nothing.

Ask Tempa (Pro): Ask what to do next, what to drop, or where something could
fit — and get an answer that already knows what's on your day.

Focus Sessions: A dial-based timer with a gentle nudge if you wander off, never
a scolding one. A session survives an app switch, a phone call, or a full
restart — you come back to exactly where you were.

Built Around Your Rhythm: Tell Tempa when you wake up and when your energy dips.
Demanding work stays in your good hours; the dip gets the light stuff.

Your Week, Without the Report Card: See your golden hours as a heat map, your
focus minutes, and what you planned against what you did — as information, never
a verdict.

Yours, Everywhere: Your day syncs privately across your iPhone and iPad through
your own iCloud account. No sign-up, no ads, no tracking.

Nine Languages: English, German, Ukrainian, Spanish, French, Portuguese,
Norwegian, Finnish and Dutch — with dates and times in your own region's format.

Plan out loud. Do one thing. Move at your tempo.
